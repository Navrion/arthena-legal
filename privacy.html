Privacy Policy for Arthena

Effective date: 5 September 2026

Arthena is an iOS app provided by Navrion. On the App Store it is listed as Arthena (bundle ID com.navrion.arthena).

Data we collect

We collect only what we need to run the product:

  • Account. If you sign in, we store the email address and user identifier from Firebase Authentication or Sign in with Apple.
  • Saved scenarios. Calculator inputs and results you choose to save, stored on-device and optionally synced with Firebase when you are signed in.
  • Purchases. Apple and RevenueCat tell us whether your Apple ID has an active Pro entitlement. We do not store your full payment card details.

How we use it

We use this data to operate the app: authentication, syncing saved scenarios, restoring purchases, and unlocking Pro features.

We do not sell your data. We do not use advertising SDKs or cross-app tracking.

How long we keep it

Saved scenarios stay until you delete them in the app. Signing out removes the local session. To request deletion of a cloud account, email support.

Third-party services

These processors see only what they need to do their job:

  • Apple — Sign in with Apple and App Store billing.
  • Firebase — Authentication and optional scenario sync (Firestore).
  • RevenueCat — Confirms whether Pro is active for your Apple ID.

Your choices

You can delete saved scenarios in Settings. You can sign out in Settings. For account deletion or an export of your data, email support.

Children

Arthena is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from them.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact: support.navrion@gmail.com
